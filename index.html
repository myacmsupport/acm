MT5 can show you the charts. It can't tell you which ones matter.

Charts. Indicators. Symbols. Timeframes. They are all there — but the burden is still on you to open the right chart, check the right timeframe, and work out what deserves attention.

Too many charts. Too much manual checking.

You can tile charts and load indicators natively — that part's fine. What's missing is anything that looks at your whole list of instruments and tells you which ones need attention. So you still open each chart yourself, on every timeframe you care about, one at a time.

Rewind multiple markets at the same time

Step backward through price history and replay it forward, with your indicators recalculating candle by candle — no look-ahead, no repainting. The Market Indicator Scanner is paused and hidden during replay so it can never report live figures beside a historical chart.

One replay clock. Multiple markets.

For example, Pro+ can replay US30 M30, XAUUSD M15, DXY D1 and EURUSD H1 simultaneously, with one shared replay clock.

  • Step back through history 1 or 10 bars at a time, then play forward frame by frame or at speed
  • Configure each replay chart with any symbol and timeframe
  • One replay clock keeps all charts synchronized, even across different symbols and timeframes
  • Replay supports up to 1 / 2 / 4 / 6 charts depending on your plan
  • See exactly when a verdict flips, not just that it did

Six other ways to look at the same market

Every panel can be set to candles, line, or one of the specialist styles below. Candles, line, line+markers and step line all use real time-based prices, so the scanner and indicators work on them normally. Heikin-Ashi, Renko and Kagi rebuild the chart from averaged prices or price movement instead of time — genuinely useful for reading trend structure, but display-only, so the scanner switches off automatically on those three.

Plus standard candlestick charts on every plan. All plans include all 21 timeframes: M1, M2, M3, M4, M5, M6, M10, M12, M15, M20, M30, H1, H2, H3, H4, H6, H8, H12, D1, W1 and MN1.

Do the scanner and indicators work on every chart type?

On candles, line, line+markers and step line — yes, fully. Heikin-Ashi, Renko and Kagi are display-only chart types: they're built from averaged prices or price movement rather than real traded prices, so indicators and the scanner switch off automatically on those, regardless of your plan. Switch back to candles to use them again.

Does ACM place trades for me?

No. ACM never places a trade and never touches your account balance. It reads market data through your MetaTrader 5 terminal and shows you what the technical studies currently say — what you do with that is entirely up to you.

Is this financial advice, or a signal service?

No. ACM doesn't tell you what to buy or sell, and it doesn't predict the future. It summarizes what a set of established technical studies currently show, on the markets you've already chosen to follow, and flags when that picture changes.

Will it work with my broker?

If your broker gives you MetaTrader 5, yes. ACM detects whichever MT5 terminal is running on your machine and automatically works out how your broker names each instrument, so the same install works unmodified across brokers.

What do I need to run it?

A Windows PC with MetaTrader 5 installed and logged in to your broker. Installation is a normal setup wizard — no coding, no command line.
