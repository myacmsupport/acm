Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

1. Information we may receive

Information may include your name, email address, license information, purchase or subscription information, device/license activation information, and information you provide for support.

2. Technical information

ACM may process technical information needed to activate and validate a license, maintain the application, diagnose connection problems, and provide support. ACM may also process market data and read-only position information supplied by the connected MetaTrader 5 terminal for application features.

3. Trading credentials

ACM is not designed to request or store your broker password, trading account credentials, or payment-card details. Payment information is handled by the payment provider.

4. Payment provider

Checkout, payment processing, tax handling, and related payment information are handled by the payment provider under its own terms and privacy policy.

5. Use of information

Information may be used to provide licenses, process purchases, provide customer support, maintain security, troubleshoot technical problems, and improve ACM.

6. Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to provide licensing, payment, hosting, support, or other services required to operate ACM. We do not sell personal information.

7. Security and retention

Information is retained as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, legal obligations, transaction records, dispute handling, and legitimate business needs. Reasonable security measures are used, but no system can guarantee absolute security.
