Which indicators does ACM include?

ACM includes 16: EMA 21/50/200, SMA 50/100/200, Godmode RSI, VMC Cipher B, SMC Structure, MACD, S/R + Breaks, VWAP, Supertrend, MFI, Volume Profile, SMC Zones, Volume, ADX / DMI (14), Stochastic (5, 3, 3), OBV.

Can I choose which indicators the market scanner uses?

Yes. You pick the studies from 13: EMA 21/50/200, SMA 50/100/200, Godmode RSI, VMC Cipher B, SMC Structure, MACD, S/R + Breaks, VWAP, Supertrend, MFI, ADX / DMI, Stochastic and OBV. Volume Profile, SMC Zones and Volume are chart tools only. The scanner also shows a divergence table for Godmode RSI, VMC Cipher B and MFI.

How many indicators can I show at once?

It depends on the plan: 3 on Essential, 6 on Essential+, 10 on Pro and unlimited on Pro+.

Do indicators work on every chart type?

They work on candles, line, line with markers and step line. Heikin-Ashi, Renko and Kagi are display-only chart types, so indicators switch off on those.

Is an indicator reading a trading signal?

No. Indicators describe what price and volume data have done. ACM is a charting and analysis tool, not financial advice, and it never places trades.
