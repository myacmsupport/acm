MT5 multi-chart layout questions

Can MetaTrader 5 show multiple charts at once?

MT5 can tile several chart windows, but each one is a separate window you manage yourself. ACM adds a single workspace with layouts from 1x1 up to 4x4, where every panel can have its own symbol and timeframe.

How many charts can I show at once in ACM?

It depends on the plan: one chart (1x1) on Essential, up to four (2x2) on Essential+, up to nine (3x3) on Pro and up to sixteen (4x4) on Pro+.

Can each chart use a different symbol and timeframe?

Yes. Every panel can use its own instrument and its own timeframe, and all 21 timeframes from M1 to MN1 are available on every plan.

Which chart types can I use in the layout?

Candles, line, line with markers and step line are on every plan. Heikin-Ashi is included from Pro, and Renko and Kagi from Pro+. Heikin-Ashi, Renko and Kagi are display-only, so indicators and the scanner switch off on those panels.

Does ACM place trades from these charts?

No. ACM never places a trade and never touches your account. It reads market data through your MetaTrader 5 terminal and is a charting and analysis tool, not financial advice.

Does it work with any MT5 broker?

Yes, on demo or live accounts. ACM finds the running MetaTrader 5 terminal and matches your broker's symbol names automatically, including suffixes.
