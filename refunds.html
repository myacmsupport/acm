Refund Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

1. Policy summary

ACM is digital software delivered electronically. All purchases, including perpetual licenses (Essential, Essential+, Pro and Pro+) and the 30-Day Pro Trial, are non-refundable, except in the limited cases in section 4 or where the law requires otherwise.

2. Before you buy

Please check that your computer, Windows, MetaTrader 5 and broker setup meet your needs, and that you understand the features and limits of your plan. The optional 30-Day Pro Trial lets you evaluate ACM before buying a perpetual license. It is a one-time $9.99 purchase that does not renew, is not credited toward any other purchase and is itself non-refundable. Choosing not to use the trial does not create a right to a refund.

3. When refunds are not provided

Refunds are not provided for:

  • change of mind;
  • not using the trial or your license;
  • misunderstanding of features or plan limits;
  • incompatibility with your computer, Windows, MetaTrader 5 or broker setup, including symbol naming;
  • insufficient broker data or history;
  • ACM’s output not matching your expectations, or trading results or losses. ACM is analysis software, not a trading adviser.

4. Limited exceptions

Technical defect. A refund may be considered where a reproducible defect in ACM itself prevents it from functioning substantially as described and the issue cannot be resolved with reasonable technical support. Contact support@acmscope.com with your purchase email and order details, and allow us to troubleshoot the problem.

Duplicate purchase. If the same plan was purchased more than once by mistake, contact support promptly and we will review the order.

5. Compatibility

ACM requires Windows and MetaTrader 5. Compatibility with every broker, Windows configuration, terminal installation or symbol naming convention cannot be guaranteed. Instrument availability and historical data depth depend on your broker.

6. Payment disputes

Please contact support before filing a payment dispute or chargeback. Unresolved chargebacks may result in license suspension while the dispute is reviewed.

7. Payment provider and your rights

Payments are processed by Gumroad and its terms also apply. Nothing in this policy excludes or limits any consumer right that cannot lawfully be excluded.
