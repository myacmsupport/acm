Refund Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

1. Contact us first

For refund requests or purchase problems, contact my.acmsupport@gmail.com with your purchase email and order information. Payment-provider rules may also apply.

2. Subscription cancellations

Cancellation normally prevents future recurring charges; it does not automatically create a refund for a charge that has already occurred.

3. Pro trial

The Pro trial is $9.99 for 7 days and then $29/month unless cancelled. If Pro Perpetual for $499 is purchased, the $9.99 trial payment is intended to be credited toward that purchase, subject to the payment provider's supported mechanism and applicable terms.

4. Duplicate or incorrect purchases

Contact support promptly if the same plan is purchased more than once. We will review the order and, where appropriate and permitted by the payment provider, arrange a correction or refund.

5. Technical problems

If ACM cannot be installed, activated, or used because of a reproducible ACM-side problem, contact support before initiating a payment dispute. We will make reasonable efforts to troubleshoot the issue.

6. Compatibility

ACM requires Windows and MetaTrader 5 for its live market-data workflow. Compatibility with every broker, Windows configuration, terminal installation, or symbol convention cannot be guaranteed.

7. Payment disputes

Please contact support before filing a chargeback where practical. Unresolved chargebacks may result in license suspension while the dispute is reviewed.

8. Payment-provider terms

The payment provider's refund, payment, and transaction terms also apply. Mandatory consumer rights are not excluded.
